How it works

Three steps, every day, automatically.

Boili switches the coil of your heater's contactor and nothing else. The heater's own safety devices stay in charge.

01

Reads the prices

Over WiFi, the ESP32 fetches tomorrow's hourly spot prices for Finland as soon as they're published.

02

Picks the cheapest hours

It schedules the heating window you need, usually the night dip, and lights the HEAT LED while it runs.

03

Switches the contactor

A relay energises your heater's contactor. The thermostat and limiter still decide when the water is hot enough.

Features

Small box, sensible decisions.

Boost button

Need a shower now? Press BOOST on the lid to heat immediately, whatever the price.

Fallback schedule

If WiFi or the price feed is down, Boili falls back to a fixed night schedule. You never wake up to cold water.

Wood-fire aware

Got a wood boiler too? An optional temperature probe lets Boili skip buying electricity when the fire has already heated the tank.

Updates over WiFi

Firmware updates arrive over the air, with no need to open the box.

6 mm isolation

The 230 V side and the low-voltage side are kept at least 6 mm apart on the PCB, and design rules check it.

Two status LEDs

HEAT and STATUS shine through light pipes in the lid, so you can see what it's doing at a glance.

Wall mount

Four slotted ears and two cable glands. It goes next to the heater's existing contactor.

No app required

Set it up once over WiFi. After that it just works, every day.

Engineering

Designed end to end in one conversation.

The schematic, PCB, enclosure, renders and videos were all made by Claude Opus 5.5 with KiCad 9, FreeCAD 1.0 and Blender, and checked with each tool's own design checks.

An isolated HLK-PM01 supply, an AMS1117 regulator, an ESP32-WROOM-32E and a relay driver with a flyback diode, on a two-layer board of 96.5 × 62 mm with a ground plane on the bottom.

Installation

Next to your heater's contactor.

Boili controls the contactor coil, so the heater current never passes through the box. Two cable glands bring in mains and run out to the coil. The thermostat and over-heat limiter stay in series with the element, so they can always switch it off.

⚠ Mains wiring and contactor installation must be done by a qualified electrician.

FAQ

Questions

Can I actually buy Boili?

No. Boili is a concept made to show Claude Opus 5.5's abilities in electronics, mechanical design and media production. The prices, ordering and availability on this page are illustrative only.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Boili keeps the last schedule it received. If it has none, it falls back to heating at a fixed time overnight, so the tank is never left cold.

Does it replace my thermostat?

No. Boili only decides when heating is allowed. The tank's own thermostat and over-heat limiter stay in series and still decide whether the element runs.

I also heat with wood. Will it waste electricity?

With the optional tank probe, Boili sees that the water is already hot and skips the electric heating window.

How was this made?

In one conversation with Claude Opus 5.5. It designed the circuit and routed the PCB in KiCad, checked them, modelled the injection-moulded enclosure in FreeCAD with a clearance check, rendered the 3D animation in Blender, and wrote this page.
